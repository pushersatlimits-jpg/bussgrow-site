What Mailroom does on your instruction

Mailroom acts on your Gmail only when you ask it to. Depending on what you do, it may:

  • send messages and replies, and save drafts;
  • apply and remove labels, archive, and move mail to Trash (Mailroom never permanently deletes mail);
  • create Gmail filters, for example when you Block a sender in Screener (their future mail goes to Trash) or turn on Auto-Archive in Sender Cleanup;
  • change your vacation responder and signatures;
  • save your RSVP to a calendar invitation, after which Google notifies the organizer;
  • send a one-click unsubscribe request to a sender when you click Unsubscribe.

You are responsible for the actions you take in Mailroom and for the messages you send. Changes made in Gmail, such as filters, labels and settings, are stored in your Google account and stay there even if you stop using Mailroom or remove your account from it.

Send Later and reminders

Send Later saves your message as a Gmail draft, and Mailroom sends it at the time you chose. This only works while your Mac is on and Mailroom is running. If it isn't, the message is sent the next time Mailroom runs. Snooze and Follow-up Reminders work the same way: they come back when Mailroom is running.

Because of this, we don't guarantee that a message will be sent, or a reminder will appear, at an exact time. Don't rely on Mailroom for anything where timing is critical.
